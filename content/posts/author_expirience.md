There are plenty of online guides that tell you a step-by-step guide to self 
publish, but not many of them truly explain what it is like to actually write one; 
the fun parts, the difficult times and everything in between.  <br>
I hope to provide a vague sense of this journey and what to look forward to (for
better or for worse) while doing it.

## The Writing

If you have an idea, <i>don't rush the process.</i> <br>
The single best thing that I unintentionally did in this project was planning. In fact,
I would go as far as saying that <i> I don't think I would have finished </i> if I didn't do one. <br> <br>


As honestly as possible, thinking ahead of your current timeline 
is inevitable and expected--formatting, cover design, publishing--but nothing matters 
more than the present.

## Portfoio Navigation

The parts worth knowing about, in rough order of how often you will use them:

- **Posts** with an optional table of contents that tracks your scroll position.
- **⌘K search** over every post's title, description and tags — fetched on the
  first keystroke, never on first paint.
- **Two independent switches**: light/dark, and a warm brown or deep green
  ground. That is four palettes, and both choices are remembered.
- **A projects section** and **a bookshelf**, to see my interests.
- **A virtual resume** rendered rather than hand-written.
